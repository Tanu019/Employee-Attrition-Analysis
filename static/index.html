Key Empirical Findings & Strategic Insights

Executive summary of empirical evidence synthesized from the 2,500 employee workforce audit:

#1 Attrition Catalyst: Burnout & Overtime
Employees who exited exhibited an average BurnoutRiskScore of 58.4 compared to 47.8 among stayers (+10.6 point divergence). Staff working frequent overtime (>30 hrs/month) experience a 1.88x exit multiplier.
#2 High-Risk Vulnerability Cohorts
Turnover is intensely concentrated in frontline operations: Delivery Operations (24.8%) and Sales (23.2%). Early career tenures (1–3 years) represent 48.7% of all departures due to competitive market poaching.
#3 The Accuracy Paradox Exposed
A naive Dummy baseline achieves 82.0% Accuracy by predicting "No Exit" for all staff, yet fails to catch a single departure (Recall = 0%). The production Random Forest is calibrated with F1 = 0.459 and ROC-AUC = 0.745.
#4 High Financial ROI of Retention
Replacing a departed employee costs ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000+. Deploying targeted wellbeing check-ins and overtime caps at ₹1,800/employee yields a 1,894% net financial return, saving over ₹48 Lakhs annually.

HR Business Implications of Errors

• False Negatives (48 Missed Exits): Costliest business error in HR. High-value employees depart unexpectedly, resulting in replacement costs (₹1,00,000+), recruitment delays, and operational friction.


• False Positives (51 False Alarms): Loyal employees flagged as potential exits. Resulting cost is minor: conducting proactive 1-on-1 check-ins, recognition, or mentoring that benefits morale regardless.


• Decision Strategy: The Random Forest achieves 45.2% Precision and 46.7% Recall, yielding the optimal F1-score balance to maximize retention ROI without overwhelming HR capacity.

Methodology Dossier

1. Business Problem Definition

Workforce attrition creates severe economic and cultural disruption. The objective of this system is to deploy supervised machine learning to detect early patterns of disengagement while strictly adhering to data leakage protocols and fair AI guidelines.

2. Data Science Workflow & Split Hygiene

• Partitioning: The dataset is split into an 80% training set (2,000 records) and a 20% held-out test set (500 records) using stratified sampling.
• Validation: Candidate models undergo 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation strictly inside the training split. Imputation and one-hot encoding are fitted only on training folds to prevent data leakage.
• Final Evaluation: Metrics presented to decision makers reflect strictly the 500 unseen test records.

3. Evaluation Metric Selection

Because the dataset exhibits an 18.0% attrition rate, Accuracy is deceptive (the Accuracy Paradox). Recall measures the percentage of genuine exits successfully caught, while Precision measures the reliability of flags. F1-score provides the harmonic mean. The Random Forest was selected as the optimal production pipeline with F1 = 0.459 and ROC-AUC = 0.745.

4. Limitations & Future Scope

• Historical cross-sectional snapshots cannot capture longitudinal real-time sentiment fluctuations.
• Recommendations represent organizational starting points, not causal guarantees.
• Future enhancements could incorporate NLP sentiment analysis of open-ended employee pulse surveys and live HRIS integrations.

SnackRush Analytics • System & Methodology Guide

1. Business Problem & Quick-Commerce Context

SnackRush operates 48 dark stores with 2,500 full-time staff across Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR. Under strict 10–15 min fulfillment SLAs, employee turnover costs ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000+ per exit. This decision support system shifts HR from reactive exit interviews to proactive, evidence-based retention.

2. Data Science Workflow & Split Hygiene

• Strict Split Hygiene: 80% Training (2,000 records) / 20% Held-Out Test (500 records) with stratified sampling.
• Zero Data Leakage: Post-outcome columns (ExitDate, ExitType, AttritionCategory, EmploymentStatus) are permanently excluded from model training.
• Fair AI: Protected demographics (Gender, Age, MaritalStatus) are excluded from operational inference.

3. The Accuracy Paradox in Imbalanced Classification

With an 18.0% attrition baseline, a naive dummy model predicting "No Attrition" achieves 82.0% Accuracy, yet catches 0 exits (Recall = 0%). Candidate algorithms are evaluated primarily on F1-score, Recall, and PR-AUC. The Random Forest Ensemble was selected as production champion (F1: 0.459, ROC-AUC: 0.745, PR-AUC: 0.483).
