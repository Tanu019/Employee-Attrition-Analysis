HR Business Error Tradeoffs

• False Negatives (48 Missed Exits): Costliest business error in HR. High-value employees depart unexpectedly, incurring replacement costs (₹1,00,000+), recruitment delays, and operational friction.


• False Positives (51 False Alarms): Loyal employees flagged as potential exits. Resulting cost is minor: conducting proactive 1-on-1 check-ins or mentorship benefits engagement regardless.


• Operational Balance: Random Forest delivers 45.2% Precision and 46.7% Recall, striking the optimal F1 balance for HR intervention capacity.

MBA Capstone Methodology Dossier

1. Business Problem Definition

Workforce attrition creates severe economic and cultural disruption. The objective of this system is to deploy supervised machine learning to detect early patterns of disengagement while strictly adhering to data leakage protocols and fair AI guidelines.

2. Data Science Workflow & Split Hygiene

• Partitioning: The dataset is split into an 80% training set (2,000 records) and a 20% held-out test set (500 records) using stratified sampling.
• Validation: Candidate models undergo 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation strictly inside the training split. Imputation and one-hot encoding are fitted only on training folds to prevent data leakage.
• Final Evaluation: Metrics presented reflect strictly the 500 unseen test records.

3. Evaluation Metric Selection & The Accuracy Paradox

Because the dataset exhibits an 18.0% attrition baseline, Accuracy is deceptive. Recall measures the percentage of genuine exits caught, while Precision measures the reliability of flags. F1-score provides the balanced harmonic mean. The Random Forest was selected as champion with F1 = 0.459 and ROC-AUC = 0.745.

SnackRush Analytics • System & Methodology Guide

1. Business Problem & Quick-Commerce Context

SnackRush operates 48 dark stores with 2,500 full-time staff across Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR. Under strict 10–15 min fulfillment SLAs, employee turnover costs ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000+ per exit. This decision support system shifts HR from reactive exit interviews to proactive, evidence-based retention.

2. Data Science Workflow & Split Hygiene

• Strict Split Hygiene: 80% Training (2,000 records) / 20% Held-Out Test (500 records) with stratified sampling.
• Zero Data Leakage: Post-outcome columns (ExitDate, ExitType, AttritionCategory, EmploymentStatus) are permanently excluded from model training.
• Fair AI: Protected demographics (Gender, Age, MaritalStatus) are excluded from operational inference.

3. The Accuracy Paradox in Imbalanced Classification

With an 18.0% attrition baseline, a naive dummy model predicting "No Attrition" achieves 82.0% Accuracy, yet catches 0 exits (Recall = 0%). Candidate algorithms are evaluated primarily on F1-score, Recall, and PR-AUC. The Random Forest Ensemble was selected as production champion (F1: 0.459, ROC-AUC: 0.745, PR-AUC: 0.483).

Ethical AI, Privacy & Data Governance Standard

Core Governance Principle: People Intelligence is an advisory decision support system. It must never be used for punitive employment actions, termination decisions, or discriminatory profiling.

What the Public & General Stakeholders CAN See:

  • Macro Workforce Statistics: Total employee counts, overall attrition percentage (18.0%), and average organizational tenure.
  • Department Comparison Trends: Relative turnover rates across business units (e.g., Delivery Ops 24.8% vs. Legal 9.2%).
  • Model Accuracy & Audit Metrics: Held-out test performance, ROC-AUC, F1-scores, and honest comparison with baseline dummy models.
  • Global Feature Rankings: Permutation importance showing systemic risk factors (overtime burden, burnout scores).
  • Evidence-Based Retention Roadmaps: Strategic interventions, manager training, and financial ROI simulator models.

What the Public CANNOT See (Redacted / Strictly Confidential):

  • Identifiable Employee PII: Names, personal phone numbers, emails, and home addresses are never published. Employee IDs are masked (e.g. EMP-***142).
  • Public Individual Flight Risk Labels: Openly labeling a specific named person as "High Flight Risk" is strictly forbidden to prevent managerial bias, retaliation, or withheld promotions.
  • Exact Individual Compensation: Individual salaries are private personnel records. The public view displays anonymized salary bands only.
  • Protected Demographic Variables in ML: Gender, Age, and Marital Status are excluded from machine learning operational features to guarantee zero algorithmic discrimination.
  • Offboarding Grievance Records: Specific exit interview allegations or interpersonal disputes involving managers.
Regulatory Compliance Standards:

Conforms with GDPR Article 22 (Human-in-the-loop oversight on automated decision systems), the India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (Purpose limitation & privacy by design), and EEOC Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures.
