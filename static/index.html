Project Background & Methodology Dossier

SnackRush Enterprise Solutions • Workforce Analytics Case Study

Governance Standard

1. Company Background

SnackRush Enterprise Solutions is a quick-commerce and on-demand logistics network focused on speed, reliability, and operational efficiency across dark stores and regional hubs. Its workforce supports fulfillment operations, dispatch routing, engineering, merchant partnerships, and frontline logistics. Attrition Lens analyzes employee workforce patterns, diagnoses attrition catalysts, and evaluates data-driven interventions to support proactive retention decisions.

2. Business Problem

Unplanned employee attrition creates severe operational and financial friction across workforce-intensive sectors. Turnover disrupts operational consistency, strains remaining personnel with overtime demands, incurs heavy recruitment and onboarding costs (often estimated at 50% to 150% of annual compensation per departed worker), and degrades institutional domain knowledge. Understanding why departures happen allows organizations to proactively address retention before resignation notices occur.

3. Project Objectives

  • Analyze employee attrition patterns: Uncover systemic historical turnover distributions across operating departments and functional roles.
  • Explore differences across available workforce attributes: Assess how tenure, compensation tiers, satisfaction ratings, and overtime exposure correlate with departures.
  • Develop and evaluate an attrition prediction model: Train, benchmark, and optimize supervised machine learning pipelines using stratified 5-fold cross-validation.
  • Understand the factors associated with model predictions: Ensure model transparency through global permutation importance and individualized feature risk attribution.
  • Translate analytical findings into practical retention recommendations: Deliver evidence-based managerial roadmaps, financial ROI simulations, and persistent action planning.

4. Analytical Approach

• Data Preparation & Preprocessing: Handled using Scikit-learn ColumnTransformer. Numerical features undergo median imputation and standard scaling; categorical features undergo one-hot encoding with rare category safeguards. Demographic proxies (Age, Gender, Marital Status) are strictly excluded from prediction features.
• Split Hygiene: The verified dataset (2,500 records) is partitioned into an 80% training set (2,000 records) and a 20% held-out test partition (500 records) using stratified sampling to preserve the 18.0% attrition baseline.
• Model Training: Candidate algorithms (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) are trained with 5-Fold Stratified Cross-Validation strictly inside the training partition.
• Evaluation Metrics: Due to class imbalance, accuracy is audited against a majority-class Dummy baseline. Production model selection prioritizes ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Precision, Recall, and F1-Score on unseen held-out test data.
• Explainability: Permutation importance computes the decrease in model score when individual features are permuted, ensuring verifiable operational explanations.

5. Limitations & Ethical Safeguards

• Probabilistic Estimates vs. Actual Outcomes: Model outputs represent calibrated departure probabilities based on historical cohort indicators; they do not dictate individual human decisions or certainty.
• Correlation vs. Causation: Statistical associations (e.g., between overtime burden and leaver status) identify high-risk signals but do not establish deterministic causation without controlled organizational interventions.
• Human-in-the-Loop Governance: Algorithmic scores are strictly advisory decision-support tools. They must never be used for punitive employment actions, compensation reductions, or termination. All interventions require empathetic, qualified managerial discretion.

SnackRush Analytics • System & Methodology Guide

1. Business Problem & Quick-Commerce Context

SnackRush operates 48 dark stores with 2,500 full-time staff across Mumbai, Bengaluru, Pune, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR. Under strict 10–15 min fulfillment SLAs, employee turnover costs ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000+ per exit. This decision support system shifts HR from reactive exit interviews to proactive, evidence-based retention.

2. Data Science Workflow & Split Hygiene

• Strict Split Hygiene: 80% Training (2,000 records) / 20% Held-Out Test (500 records) with stratified sampling.
• Zero Data Leakage: Post-outcome columns (ExitDate, ExitType, AttritionCategory, EmploymentStatus) are permanently excluded from model training.
• Fair AI: Protected demographics (Gender, Age, MaritalStatus) are excluded from operational inference.

3. The Accuracy Paradox in Imbalanced Classification

With an 18.0% attrition baseline, a naive dummy model predicting "No Attrition" achieves 82.0% Accuracy, yet catches 0 exits (Recall = 0%). Candidate algorithms are evaluated primarily on F1-score, Recall, and PR-AUC. The Random Forest Ensemble was selected as production champion (F1: 0.459, ROC-AUC: 0.745, PR-AUC: 0.483).

Ethical AI, Privacy & Data Governance Standard

Core Governance Principle: People Intelligence is an advisory decision support system. It must never be used for punitive employment actions, termination decisions, or discriminatory profiling.

What the Public & General Stakeholders CAN See:

  • Macro Workforce Statistics: Total employee counts, overall attrition percentage (18.0%), and average organizational tenure.
  • Department Comparison Trends: Relative turnover rates across business units (e.g., Delivery Ops 24.8% vs. Legal 9.2%).
  • Model Accuracy & Audit Metrics: Held-out test performance, ROC-AUC, F1-scores, and honest comparison with baseline dummy models.
  • Global Feature Rankings: Permutation importance showing systemic risk factors (overtime burden, burnout scores).
  • Evidence-Based Retention Roadmaps: Strategic interventions, manager training, and financial ROI simulator models.

What the Public CANNOT See (Redacted / Strictly Confidential):

  • Identifiable Employee PII: Names, personal phone numbers, emails, and home addresses are never published. Employee IDs are masked (e.g. EMP-***142).
  • Public Individual Flight Risk Labels: Openly labeling a specific named person as "High Flight Risk" is strictly forbidden to prevent managerial bias, retaliation, or withheld promotions.
  • Exact Individual Compensation: Individual salaries are private personnel records. The public view displays anonymized salary bands only.
  • Protected Demographic Variables in ML: Gender, Age, and Marital Status are excluded from machine learning operational features to guarantee zero algorithmic discrimination.
  • Offboarding Grievance Records: Specific exit interview allegations or interpersonal disputes involving managers.
Regulatory Compliance Standards:

Conforms with GDPR Article 22 (Human-in-the-loop oversight on automated decision systems), the India Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (Purpose limitation & privacy by design), and EEOC Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures.
